Fig. 237 shows a two-dimensional vertical cross-section.

A horizontal mirror lies along the bottom.
A vertical screen stands on the mirror to the right.
An upright oblong object stands on the mirror between the incoming rays and the screen.
The object is represented in the cross-section as a vertical segment with its lower endpoint on the mirror.
Sunrays are represented by mutually parallel straight rays incident obliquely downward toward the horizontal mirror.
The rays are reflected from the horizontal mirror and the reflected rays propagate toward the vertical screen.
The object lies horizontally between at least some incident/reflected ray paths and the screen.
